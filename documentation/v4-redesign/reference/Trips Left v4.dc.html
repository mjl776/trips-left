A fantasy football optimizer that uses advanced metrics the average fan might not look at to help you identify the key players in your lineup. Build a lineup against your league's real scoring settings and see which players are carrying you, which are dead weight, and which are quietly worth a flier.

"Trips left" is a formation: three receivers bunched on the left side of the offense, one on the right. The name is about alignment — figuring out what your team is actually built to do, then lining it up that way.
